# Can 12 Fit at Citizens Kitchen & Bar for a 7 AM Breakfast on August 5?

**Question:** I want to take 12 people to Citizens Kitchen & Bar, Mandalay Bay, Las Vegas, NV, on 5 August at 0700. Is it big enough to fit all of us?

## Bradlee — So, what's the answer?

Practically, yes — but not by walking in. Citizens Kitchen & Bar doesn't publish a seating-capacity number, and it turns out that number isn't actually what determines your answer. The restaurant's own policy is what matters: Citizens doesn't take reservations for ordinary dining, but it draws an explicit line at **groups of 12 or more**, which require advance arrangement through its events contact (`lasvegasevents@sbe.com`) rather than a normal walk-in seating. Your clarified headcount — 12 total, including you — lands exactly on that line. So "is it big enough" isn't really a square-footage question here; it's a "have you called ahead" question, and the restaurant is telling you, in its own stated policy, that a party your size needs to.

The timing adds one more wrinkle worth flagging: Citizens' breakfast service runs 7:00 AM–11:00 AM, so 0700 is the restaurant's opening minute for breakfast, not a comfortably-settled-in mid-morning slot. Showing up right at open with a 12-person party that hasn't been pre-arranged is a real, avoidable risk — not because the room is too small, but because nothing has been confirmed with staff in advance for that specific time.

The date is worth noting too, even though you didn't ask about it: August 5, 2026 is a Wednesday, and a prior Nexus report confirms that's the day of your own Black Hat book signing (2:00 PM, per `2026-07-27-blackhat-book-signing-schedule.md`). This breakfast, at 7 AM the same morning, is very plausibly a group breakfast ahead of that day — worth flagging as a reasonable inference, not a confirmed fact, since you didn't state the occasion.

**Recommended next step:** email `lasvegasevents@sbe.com` before August 5 to confirm they can seat your group of 12 at 7:00 AM specifically — don't rely on this report's "yes" as a substitute for that confirmation, since the whole reason your party size matters is that the restaurant itself says this size needs to be arranged, not walked in.

One thing this report couldn't fully pin down: the restaurant's phone number appears differently across three independent sources (see Sherlock and Popper below) — use the email channel above rather than guessing at the right number.
