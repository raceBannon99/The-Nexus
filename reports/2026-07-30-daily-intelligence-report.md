## Critical Infrastructure & Nation-State Policy

**[China and Iran Already Inside US Grids: CISA Demands Tested Isolation Plans](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/321930/20260729/state-hackers-already-inside-cisa-demands-pre-built-infrastructure-isolation-plans.htm)** (Tech Times, reporting on a joint framework from CISA, Australia's ASD, UK's NCSC, and Canada's Cyber Centre) — The "CI Fortify" framework, published July 28, tells critical infrastructure operators isolation from third-party dependencies, telecom, internet, vendors, and upstream services must be pre-engineered and tested before a conflict, not improvised during one. The advisory is explicit about why: Iran-linked IRGC units have shown willingness to deploy data-wiping malware simultaneously against multiple targets, while China's Volt Typhoon spent at least five years undetected inside a US critical infrastructure network and a second group, Salt Typhoon, burrowed into nine major US telecom carriers (including AT&T, Verizon, and Lumen) and accessed law-enforcement wiretap systems.

**[Cyberattack hits Angola's largest telco hours before landmark stock debut](https://therecord.media/angola-unitel-cyberattack-outage)** (The Record) — Unitel, Angola's largest telecom operator with 21+ million subscribers, was hit by a cyberattack around 2 a.m. local time that knocked out voice, mobile data, and internet nationwide — less than 24 hours before its debut on Angola's stock exchange, the largest IPO in the country's capital-market history. Network telemetry suggests the disruption came from an internal-systems failure rather than an external DDoS (the company's internet routing stayed up throughout). Trading proceeded anyway, valuing the company at $2.14 billion; no attribution or comment on timing from Angolan authorities as of publication.

**[Critical Cisco FMC Zero-Day Actively Exploited](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/cisco-fmc-zero-day-actively-exploited.html)** (Hacker News) — CVE-2026-20316, hardcoded low-privilege credentials in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center Software, is being actively exploited to access sensitive data without authentication; Cisco confirmed exploitation began earlier this month.

**[Three Critical VMware Flaws Allow Auth Bypass, Code Execution, and VM Escape](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/three-critical-vmware-flaws-allow-auth.html)** (Hacker News, Broadcom advisory) — Five vulnerabilities patched across ESXi, vCenter, Workstation, and Fusion (CVSS 2.7–9.8), the most severe being CVE-2026-47876, a VM-escape flaw in the VMXNET3 virtual network adapter letting a VM administrator execute code on the underlying ESXi host. No confirmed in-the-wild exploitation as of the July 29 advisory.

**[Military jamming disrupted a medical plane's GPS system before it crashed last month in New Mexico](https://abcnews.com/US/wireStory/military-jamming-disrupted-medical-planes-gps-system-crashed-134008225)** (AP via ABC News, also covered by Wired) — An NTSB preliminary report found active military GPS jamming near White Sands Missile Range caused a medical air-ambulance to lose navigation and crash into a mountainside near Lincoln, New Mexico on May 14, killing both pilots and two flight nurses. Air traffic control had the military pause jamming after multiple aircraft reported GPS loss, then authorized it to resume once the crew reported the airport in sight — shortly before impact. A stark illustration of military electronic-warfare capability creating unintended civilian critical-infrastructure risk.

## Nation-State Activity

**[Russian Hackers Exploit Microsoft OWA Flaw to Keep Mailbox Access After Credential Rotation](https://therecord.media/russia-hackers-outlook-webmail-malware)** (The Record and Hacker News, both citing Proofpoint research — consolidated as one story) — Laundry Bear (aka Void Blizzard, TA488 — linked by U.S. prosecutors to Russian IT firm Yutek-NN and the FSB) began exploiting a since-patched Outlook Web Access flaw (CVE-2026-42897) a day *before* the July 23 international alert about its separate Zimbra campaign — meaning the group was more active over the prior months than first understood. The novel "half-click" infection chain (simply opening an email triggers it) delivers a new browser-based implant, OWAReaper, that establishes server-side persistence surviving credential rotation and even full device re-imaging; targets include US/European government, telecom, financial, hospitality, and aerospace organizations. Proofpoint called it the most sophisticated half-click-delivered backdoor it has observed.

**[Amazon Links Debug and Chalk npm Hijack to North Korea's Sapphire Sleet](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/amazon-links-debug-and-chalk-npm-hijack.html)** (Hacker News, Amazon Threat Intelligence) — Amazon formally tied three previously separate npm supply-chain compromises — typo-crypto (March 2025), debug and chalk (September 2025), and axios (March 2026) — to a single DPRK-linked cluster tracked as Sapphire Sleet/BlueNoroff/CageyChameleon. Rather than one flagged malicious package, the operators fragment their tooling across multiple "normal-looking" libraries using trojanized post-install hooks and shared TTPs across campaigns.

**[Hackers Exploit AnySign4PC via Hacked Korean Sites to Install Backdoors Without Prompts](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/hackers-exploit-anysign4pc-via-hacked.html)** (Hacker News) — Attackers compromised trusted South Korean websites to silently trigger a buffer-overflow flaw in AnySign4PC, a certificate-based e-signature tool, delivering SIGNBT and COPPERHEDGE backdoors without any user interaction. The advisory calls the campaign state-sponsored without formal attribution, though a separate March 2026 AnySign4PC attack was attributed by AhnLab to the Lazarus Group.
