No evidence of the claimed compromise has been provided, and there's no public evidence ASOS even uses Snowflake to send push notifications. ASOS shares fell more than 10% before the company could confirm or deny anything. Tracked as a new Cybercrime row (Tier 3, unverified) in the [[Adversary Tracking Report]].

**Ukraine:** [Ukraine grocery chain ATB confirms cyberattack as hackers threaten to leak data](https://therecord.media/atb-ukraine-cyberattack-ransomware) (The Record, Oct. 5). DataSuckers — a financially motivated, Russian-speaking group that has previously targeted Russian businesses (Dodo Pizza, Tez Tour), not a war-aligned actor — posted a countdown-timer extortion demand ($400,000) on the website of Ukraine's largest grocery retailer, claiming 7.9 million customers' names/phones/emails/addresses, employee passport data, and 11M+ order records. ATB initially called the defacement "technical maintenance" and denied a breach; DataSuckers then published data samples on Telegram. Tracked as a new Cybercrime row (Tier 2) in the [[Adversary Tracking Report]].

**Russia:** [Belarusian hacktivists spent two years inside Russian healthcare network, researchers say](https://therecord.media/belarusian-hacktivists-two-years-Russian-healthcare-network) (The Record, Oct. 5). The group accessed sensitive medical data but didn't disrupt systems, assessed as positioning for espionage and trusted-relationship attacks via the network's own inter-organization connections. Deployed malware: Vasilek, a Windows backdoor communicating via Telegram. Tracked as a new Hacktivism row (Tier 2) in the [[Adversary Tracking Report]] — the category's first new entry since 09-23.

Booba — reportedly a rebrand of the Frag ransomware operation — hit UIC's College of Medicine, taking its systems down temporarily; the university's main network and patient care at UI Health were unaffected. Roughly 344GB were stolen from college servers; UIC is investigating whether personal, research, or academic data was compromised. Tracked as a new Cybercrime row (Tier 2) in the [[Adversary Tracking Report]].

## Data Breaches

**[8.8 Million Impacted by Data Breach at Denmark's Central Person Register](https://www.securityweek.com/8-8-million-impacted-by-data-breach-at-denmarks-central-person-register/)** (SecurityWeek, Oct. 6). Hackers abused a private Danish company's lawful access to Denmark's national civil registration system (CPR) to exfiltrate names, addresses, and CPR numbers (the Danish SSN equivalent) of roughly 8.8 million people, living and deceased. Discovered Oct. 2; CPR terminated the company's access, notified the Danish Data Protection Agency, and opened an investigation with police. No threat actor named.

**[Japanese media group Nikkei discloses intrusions targeting employees and users](https://therecord.media/nikkei-cyberattack-japan-data)** (The Record, Oct. 5). Two apparently separate incidents: a compromised Microsoft 365 employee account sent roughly 9,000 phishing emails Sept. 30 to past Nikkei-employee contacts, including journalistic sources; and unauthorized access to a different employee's Google Workspace account from late July (discovered early August via a Google alert), affecting 1,646 people. No attribution for either; Nikkei hasn't determined whether the two are connected.

## Cybersecurity Zero Trust Tactics

**[Apple to Tighten Full Disk Access Controls in macOS Amid AI Risks](https://www.securityweek.com/apple-to-tighten-full-disk-access-controls-in-macos-amid-ai-risks/)** (SecurityWeek, Oct. 6). Apple announced tightened macOS Full Disk Access controls, explicitly citing growing risk from "increasingly capable and autonomous AI agents" — applications will need explicit user consent for this level of access. Context: tech journalist Jason Aten reported two weeks earlier that Meta's Muse assistant accessed his private iMessages despite his believing Full Disk Access was disabled; Meta disputes the claim. *Cross-reference: AI Singularity Timeline, above — an industry containment-tightening response to agent risk, logged there but not independently CIR-matched to that category.*

**[NVIDIA launches an Open Agent Safety Platform](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/open-agent-safety-platform)** (NVIDIA, Oct. 6 per Gmail newsletter reporting). OpenShell (open-source secure runtime boundary for AI agents, extensible beyond NVIDIA's own CPUs to Arm/Intel) plus Sentry (an independent hardware watchdog on BlueField-4 DPUs that quarantines agents exceeding their boundaries within milliseconds, enforcing zero-trust access policies) — over 100 partners including Anthropic, Cisco, CrowdStrike, Microsoft, Palantir, Palo Alto Networks, IBM, SAP, and ServiceNow. The most significant industry-wide agent-containment infrastructure commitment logged in this vault since the Singularity Forecast Report's 09-29 reseed.

**[Muse Creates Detailed Profiles of All Your Friends and Family](https://www.wired.com/story/muse-creates-detailed-profiles-of-all-your-friends-and-family/)** (Wired, Lily Hay Newman & Matt Burgess, Oct. 3). Meta's AI agent Muse builds detailed relationship/friend-and-family profiles from user data; the piece examines the privacy cost of the access the agent requests — a companion story to the Apple item above, same underlying product.
